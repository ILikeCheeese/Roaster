GRANDVAULT — how to open it
============================

GrandVault runs entirely on this computer. It never uses the internet and none
of your papers ever leave this device.

WINDOWS
-------
1. Double-click  "Start GrandVault.bat"  to open GrandVault.
   (A small window titled "GrandVault (keep open - close to quit)" appears —
    leave it alone while you use the app. Closing it quits GrandVault.)

2. To get an icon on your Desktop, double-click  "Add Desktop Icon.bat"  once.
   Then you can open GrandVault any time from the Desktop icon, and you can
   right-click that icon and choose "Pin to taskbar".

   The first time, Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box.
   Click "More info" -> "Run anyway". (This is normal for a local app.)

MAC
---
1. Double-click  "Start GrandVault.command"  to open GrandVault.
   The first time, macOS may say it's from an unidentified developer:
   right-click the file -> "Open" -> "Open".
   (If it won't run, open Terminal and type:  chmod +x "Start GrandVault.command")

2. To keep it handy, drag  "Start GrandVault.command"  onto your Dock, or make
   an alias on the Desktop (right-click -> "Make Alias").

Close the little server window (Windows) or Terminal window (Mac) to quit.

Family setup notes are in SETUP.md.
